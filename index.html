ENGINE

How RORR knows a match

An esports ontology engine links every fight, objective and gold swing so an answer can point to the moment it came from.

Three rules we keep

  1. No number without a source.If we can't show where a figure came from, we don't use it.
  2. We don't say who played badly.We say what happened in the match, not who to blame.
  3. When we're wrong, we fix it in public.Corrections stay visible with their history.
